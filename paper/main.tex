\author{mememe project}

\begin{abstract}
We study binary prediction of significant 15-minute bar direction across meme coins using
de-symbolized OHLCV features and MLP classifiers under Leave-One-Symbol-Out (LOSO) evaluation.
After systematic label ($k$) and window ($w$) tuning, \textbf{development protocol} (ratio-LOSO with
full-calendar held-out test) yields mean ROC-AUC up to $\approx 0.74$ on legacy 8-coin and
category-specific universes. Under \textbf{strict calendar walk-forward} (test only on the last
15\% of global time, $\max(\text{train}) < \min(\text{test})$), the same frozen configurations
collapse to AUC $\approx 0.51$, consistent with external holdout coins and leakage audit PASS.
We attribute the $\sim$23 percentage-point protocol gap to shared market-state co-movement
(mean pairwise return correlation 0.725), not implementation leakage. Category-stratified
experiments (five classes $\times$ 16 coins) show higher in-sample sortability for some segments
(e.g.\ base\_eco dev AUC 0.74) but no improvement in strict forward generalization.
A supplementary time-decay feature weighting ablation is reported in the appendix.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Meme coins share high volatility and retail participation, motivating cross-coin transfer learning.
Prior work often reports in-domain accuracy without strict temporal holdout. We explicitly separate:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Development protocol} (ratio-LOSO): train on pooled $N{-}1$ coins (85\% time),
        test held-out coin on \emph{all} its bars---calendar may overlap with training pool.
  \item \textbf{Strict protocol} (calendar wf7085): train $\le 70\%$ time, validate 70--85\%,
        test held-out coin only on $t > 85\%$ with no calendar overlap.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Main claim:} under strict protocol, cross-symbol 15m direction events are not predictably
sortable beyond chance ($\text{AUC} \approx 0.51$). Development AUC must not be reported as
generalization.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Task}
Input: past $w$ bars $\times$ 15 de-symbolized features (returns, candle shape, volume z-score,
volatility, RSI/MACD, taker ratios). Label: $y=1$ if $r_{t+1} > k\sigma_t$, $y=0$ if
$r_{t+1} < -k\sigma_t$, else discard. Frozen paper configs: meme8 $k{=}1.2, w{=}192$; base\_eco
peak $k{=}1.5, w{=}224$ (dev tuning only).

\subsection{Model}
MLP: flatten $(w \times F) \to$ Linear(256)--BN--ReLU--Dropout $\to$ 128 $\to$ 64 $\to$ logit;
BCEWithLogits, AdamW, early stop on val macro-F1. StandardScaler fit on train only.

\subsection{Evaluation}
Primary metric: ROC-AUC (threshold-free). LOSO: 16 or 8 rounds, mean $\pm$ std across held-out symbols.
External holdout: train legacy 8 coins + one unseen symbol under strict calendar.

\section{Experiments}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item BTC single-coin baseline ($\text{AUC} \approx 0.54$).
  \item Legacy meme8 LOSO + label/window ablations ($k \times w$ synergy).
  \item Strict calendar walk-forward on frozen meme8 config.
  \item Five research categories $\times$ 16 Binance USDT pairs (independent LOSO per category).
  \item base\_eco validation plan: strict + holdout + dev PnL (4 configs).
  \item Leakage audit (PASS); mechanism figures (return correlation, protocol timeline).
  \item Appendix: time-decay MLP vs flat MLP (strict meme8).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Mechanism}
Time-aligned 15m log returns across meme8: mean pairwise correlation 0.725 (high BTC-vol
subsample 0.782). Under development protocol, models can rank coins \emph{contemporaneously}
when sector shocks align; strict protocol removes this channel.

\subsection{Statistics (strict base\_eco)}
Bootstrap 95\% CI for mean round AUC: [0.502, 0.520] ($n{=}16$ rounds, $k{=}1.5, w{=}224$).
Economically indistinguishable from random trading rule.

\subsection{Development PnL (not generalization evidence)}
Realistic backtest (30 bps round-trip, $\tau{=}0.70$) shows positive dev-protocol returns for
base\_eco configs; invalid under strict calendar forward test.

\section{Discussion}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Negative result is informative:} unified OHLCV direction models do not transfer
        across meme coins in strict time holdout.
  \item \textbf{Protocol literacy:} $\sim$23 pp gap is driven by evaluation design + co-movement,
        not label leakage (audit PASS).
  \item \textbf{Category split:} in-sample peaks (base\_eco) do not imply deployable $\alpha$.
  \item \textbf{Future work:} event/breakout labels, regime gating, cross-category generalization
        matrix, non-OHLCV data---out of scope for this paper.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}
OHLCV-only; label uses $\sigma_t$ also present in features (sample selection, not leakage);
validation threshold tuning on val set; CNN/Transformer not exhaustively compared; four category
strict spot-checks extrapolated not re-run.

\section{Time-decay feature weighting}
Flat MLP treats all bars in the lookback equally. We test exponential decay
$w_i \propto \gamma^{T-i}$ ($\gamma{=}0.95$) before flattening on meme8 strict calendar
($k{=}1.2, w{=}192$, 8-round LOSO).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Strict calendar wf7085: flat MLP vs time-decay MLP (meme8).}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Model & Mean ROC-AUC & $\Delta$ vs flat \\
\midrule
MLP (flat) & 0.508 & --- \\
MLP + time decay ($\gamma{=}0.95$) & 0.521 $\pm$ 0.017 & +1.3 pp \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

No material improvement ($<2$ pp); strict failure is not explained by uniform temporal weighting.
